## Investigated 2026-08-14 -- packet window starts MID-INSTRUCTION

Read the retail bytes directly from the exe with pefile+capstone. The window
0x32A286/41 is NOT an instruction boundary: 0x32A286 is byte 2 of a
`push 0x12F0718` immediate whose instruction starts at 0x32A284.

The real enclosing function is 0x32A220-0x32A2AE (143 bytes, `ret` at 0x32A2AE,
int3 padding to 0x32A2E0 where evaluateHasUnits begins). functions.csv already
carries that exact span as `?d_0032a220@@YAXXZ` (143B, gen_asm -- replaceable).

It is also NOT the simple `isStructure() && !isFactionStructure()` body the
packet suggests. It opens with TWO independently-guarded lazy function-local
static KindOfMaskType constructions sharing a guard dword at 0x12F0734 (bit0
gates a 6-index ctor at 0x12F0718 with args {0,8,9,10,11,12}; bit1 gates a
4-index ctor reusing KINDOFMASK_FS storage at 0x12F06FC with args
{0,0x6C,7,0x58}). It then does `add esi,0xC8` ONCE and reuses that offset
pointer as ecx for BOTH calls to 0x497D3 -- meaning both isAnyKindOf-shaped
calls dispatch on an object-relative field at this+0xC8, NOT through
Thing::getTemplate() as the already-matched Thing::isAnyKindOf does.

Byte comparison alone cannot distinguish `this` from `this+0xC8` -- the
push/mov/call opcodes are identical either way. Only reading the binary exposed
it.
