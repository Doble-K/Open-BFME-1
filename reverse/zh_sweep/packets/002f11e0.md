## Investigated 2026-08-14 -- 33 of 38 bytes; second singleton is not the first

The body compiles to the right shape from ScriptActions.cpp. Two things stop
it, and the second is why nothing was landed.

**getTeamNamed is ScriptEngine vtable slot 26** (`call [eax+0x68]`), where
the ZH header lands it at 23. That is the same slot number
?doNamedDelete@ScriptActions@@ reaches for getUnitNamed, so whichever of the
two BFME actually puts there, the ZH slot numbering is off by three for both.
ScriptActions.cpp now carries a file-scope BFMERetailScriptEngineVTable for
exactly this.

**The two singletons differ.** Retail loads `ds:0x12F076C` for the first call
and `ds:0x12F0888` for the tail call. The ZH source routes both through
TheScriptEngine:

    Team *team = TheScriptEngine->getTeamNamed(teamName);
    TheScriptEngine->setSequentialTimer(team, waitForFrames);

so one of those two is not TheScriptEngine in BFME. DIR32 sites are masked
from the byte comparison, so this body would verify green with the ZH source
either way -- the check cannot see which global is loaded. Landing it would
bake in a call on the wrong object and prove nothing.

Compare packet 002efaf0, where the same trap was walked into and had to be
retracted: there retail loads `ds:0x12F076C` **twice**, so the tail call is on
the same singleton as getUnitNamed, and ZH's `TheGameLogic->destroyObject`
cannot be right either.

The tail target here is ILT 0x00027237 -> body **0x00381050**, 139 bytes,
unclaimed. Naming it settles which singleton 0x12F0888 is. Until then the
packet's `?setSequentialTimer@ScriptEngine@@` pin is a hypothesis, not
evidence -- and note that pinning a wrong name at a right address still
byte-verifies, so it must be checked, not assumed.
