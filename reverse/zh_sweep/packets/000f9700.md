## Investigated 2026-08-14 (twice) -- codegen-shape wall

Two independent agents reached the same wall; the second found the first's
diagnosis in reverse/re_attempts.log and confirmed it rather than re-deriving.

19 of 29 bytes match, through the call to playerFromParam and the null-check
false path. Retail's true path is
`cmp DWORD PTR [eax+0x30],0xffffffff` / `sete al` -- isSupplySourceAttacked is
fully inlined to a direct field compare against the object, not ZH's m_ai
forward.

Writing that same inline field read (raw pointer cast to the +0x30 dword, direct
equality return) still emits
`mov edx,[eax+0x30]; xor ecx,ecx; cmp edx,-1; sete cl` -- materialise-through-
register instead of compare-against-memory. That is a codegen shape, not an
identity problem.

Changing the TU's compile flags might move it but would risk the other 16 matched
rows in that shared file, so it was correctly not attempted. Needs a new idea,
not another attempt.
