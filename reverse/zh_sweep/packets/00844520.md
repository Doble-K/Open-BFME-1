## Investigated 2026-08-14 -- 25 of 26 bytes; return-shape residual

The body is STLport's `__default_constructed_aux<MultiplayerColorDefinition>`
(the map operator[] fallback, _STLP_DEFAULT_CONSTRUCTOR_BUG path, instantiated
naturally via MultiplayerSettings.cpp's m_colorList usage).

All 25 body bytes match exactly -- prologue, esi=arg, the ctor call to the
pinned callee at 0x4048A, epilogue. ONLY the final opcode differs:

- retail: `c2` (ret imm16 -- callee pops the hidden by-value-return pointer)
- ours:   `c3` (plain ret -- caller pops)

Two independent levers tried, BOTH failed:
1. A user-declared copy constructor on the real class, injected via this TU's
   own `// cl:` line with -Ireference/shims/00844520 (per lessons-archive's
   "declared copy constructor forces hidden-pointer return" note). No change to
   the target byte, and it perturbed the file's destructor EH funclet
   (uw_00bf5930), so it was reverted.
2. The same lever on an isolated local replica class, with no shared-header
   edit. Also no change -- which together with (1) rules out class-triviality
   classification as the cause.

The `push ecx / mov [esp+4],0 / call / pop ecx` shape is independently on record
as a failed lever in re_attempts.log's Get_Texture@ParticleBufferClass entry
(there the lever tried was a user destructor, also failed).

This is a calling-convention-level return-shape difference, not reachable from
source. The packet-supplied callee pin was kept -- it is correct regardless of
who lands this.
