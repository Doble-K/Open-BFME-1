The scene call needs no pin: 0x0003442D is an incremental-link thunk the ledger
already resolves to ?getADynamicLight@RTS3DScene@@QAEPAVW3DDynamicLight@@XZ, so
naming the scene class RTS3DScene in the stand-in is enough.

The two stragglers are `lea ecx,[esp+4]' and the `mov byte ptr [esi+0x148],1'.
Retail puts the lea between the ambient and diffuse stores and the enabled
store immediately after the four zeroes; we hoist the lea to just after the
null test and sink the enabled store to just before the virtual call. Four
orderings of setEnabled's body, a declared Vector3 copy constructor, by-value
Set_Ambient/Set_Diffuse and moving setEnabled after Set_Ambient all produce the
identical output. Same class of wall as the lea SIB encoding in
W3DStaticText.cpp, and not a layout question.
