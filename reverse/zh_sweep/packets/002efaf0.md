## Investigated 2026-08-14 -- the callee pin in this packet is WRONG; retracted

This packet says the tail jump target is
`?destroyObject@GameLogic@@QAEXPAVObject@@@Z` at 0x0003B26E, "already in the
ledger". Both halves of that are wrong in a way that will silently pass a
byte check, so it is worth spelling out.

`?destroyObject@GameLogic@@` IS in the ledger, at body 0x0038B0C0, reached
through ILT **0x0001D0DE**. ILT **0x0003B26E** is a different thunk
targeting a different body, **0x0033C7B0**, which has no ledger row at all.

Why that is dangerous: `add_match` resolves a REL32 call by trying every
address recorded for the symbol and keeping whichever reproduces the target
bytes. Pin the wrong symbol at the right address and the body byte-verifies
anyway -- the check confirms the displacement, not the identity. This
conversion was landed that way and has been **retracted**: the row is gone
and ScriptActions.cpp is back to the ZH body.

What the bytes actually say:

- the singleton loaded for the tail call is **the same one** getUnitNamed was
  called on -- retail loads `ds:0x12F076C` twice. Compare
  ?doTeamSpinForFramecount@ScriptActions@@ at 0x002F11E0, which loads
  `ds:0x12F076C` and then a *different* global `ds:0x12F0888`. So this body's
  second call is a method on the first singleton, not on TheGameLogic, and
  the ZH source line `TheGameLogic->destroyObject(theUnit)` is not what
  BFME does here.
- the vtable slot for the first call is 26 (`[eax+0x68]`), where the ZH
  header lands getUnitNamed at 23.

singleton as getUnitNamed. Note 0x002F11E0 reaches
